# Why did I write this program?

First, I didn't "write it" by myself.  It's the culmination of cooperative work between myself and an AI called Claude Code.
I'm not a great programmer, I learned machine language decades ago, for the 6800 and 6502 microprocessors, then taught myself
how to program in BASIC. My first computer was a home built device with a 6802 uP in it, 4K or ram, programmed in machine 
code/HEX/Binary, programs saved on cassette tape -- the whole thing built with Integrated chips, sockets and wire-wrap technology
in the late 1970s.  It was 1981 before I owned a commercial computer, an Apple ][+.

I'm a retired USAF Veteran, an electronics tech, and engineer (my second career after the military). Now, I'm just retired.

So, again, not a "Rel Programmer" and use AI stuff to help me muddle through things.

I wrote this program to be able to connect to my MUSH, and to friends' MUSHes around the world. 

I noted there were not many good Android terminal programs that didn't cost a lot of money so I decided to make this one. I'm sure 
those that exist are fine, but I didn't like them. There were few features in most that I wanted or needed, so, I decided to 
take Mike Griffith's "Potato" code and examine it. Then, since I don't know much about TCL, I considered Python, then said
"Nope, let's do this for Android, not a desktop". And yes, I KNOW people don't play on mushes much, or even know what they are,
but many do, and many are mobile now. So, why not make this for a mobile device?

# Why is it called YAM?

YAM is an acronymn for "Your Adventure Mobile" because 1) Military people use acroynmns, 2) retired military guys try not to, 
3) Potato was the program I wanted to "sorta model" and 4) I wanted to remain in the same theme of potatoes. Thus, 4) YAM it was.
If you read Mike's original FAQs you will find that he kept the name because someone else originally wrote the program.
